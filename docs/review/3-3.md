Joining `tx1.S1–tx2.S2` gives series-opposition (`Vpol < 0.5 V`); joining the trap `tx1.S1–tx2.S1` gives series-aiding (`Vpol = 2 * 115 * (Vp / 230) ≈ 230 V`) and triggers validation/live warnings.

Primary readings (`Vp, Ip, Wp`) remain unaffected by secondary current. Series-aiding join (`tx1.S1–tx2.S1` or `tx1.S2–tx2.S2`) triggers a short-circuit trip (`mcbTripped: true`). Overload warning above `110%` (`19.13 A`) and overheating `runFailed` above `125%` (`21.74 A`), matching Exp 3.1.

## Assumptions & Decisions
- **`vaS.knob` slider range (`0–30 V`, `step: 0.1`, `fineStep: 0.1`)**: While `vaS` is a `230 V / 0–270 V` auxiliary autotransformer (`params.VoutMax = 270`), setting Stage 2's `vaS.knob` slider max to `30 V` with `0.1 V` steps (same pattern as Exp 3.2 Stage 2) enables hitting the `25%` current point (`~2.3–2.4 V`, `4.35 A ± 8%`) accurately by slider, knob drag, or `findVariacForTargetV1`.
- **Stage 1 -> Stage 2 `vpol` removal & `as` placement**: `as` occupies `[1.25, 0, 0.85]` in Stage 2 after `vpol` is removed from Stage 1, keeping the bench compact and within `11.8 × 4.3` bounds while preserving `>= 0.35` spacing in both stages.
