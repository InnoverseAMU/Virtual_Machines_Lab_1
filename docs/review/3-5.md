## Parameters & Equations
- Primary: 230 V line-to-line, internal delta, 3φ autotransformer 0–270 V (`0.1 V` fine step).
- Secondary half-windings: `6 × 57.5 V` (`2 × 57.5 V = 115 V` per full phase at 230 V primary).
- Star: `Vp = 115.0 V`, `VL = √3 · 115 = 199.19 V`.
- Delta: `Vp = VL = 115.0 V`.
- Zig-Zag (Fig. 3.5.3: `a1-b1-c1` neutral, `a2-b4`, `b2-c4`, `c2-a4` legs, outputs `b3, c3, a3`): `Vp-zz = |-Ea + Eb| = √3 · 57.5 = 99.59 V`, `VL-zz = 3 · 57.5 = 172.50 V`.

## How to Check by Hand
1. Run `npm run dev` and select **Exp 3.5** (`Study of various three-phase transformer connections`).
2. **Self mode**:
   - Stage 1: Switch ON `sup3`, set `va3` to `230 V`, select `Red (+)` / `Black (-)` probes in sidebar (or click the probe jacks on the 3D meter) to test terminal pairs, record the six `57.5 V` pairs as `W1..W6` with zero bench wires, record a row, then Submit.
   - Stage 2: Wire two `W_k` pairs in series, energise to verify `0 V / 115 V` (same phase) vs `57.5 V / 99.6 V` (cross phase), assign Phases `a, b, c` (2 pairs each), leave all 3 phases series-aiding (`115 V`), record `Vp`, then Submit to reveal `a1..c4`.
   - Stages 3–5: Wire Star (`199.2 V`), Delta (`115 V` — also verify reversing one Delta phase trips the 3φ MCB), and Zig-Zag Fig. 3.5.3 (`99.6 V` / `172.5 V`), record rows at `230 V`, inspect reference phasor diagrams, and submit the report.
3. **Learn mode / Observe mode**: Verify Stage 1–5 guided steps and pre-wired Observe stages with `a1..c4` resolved labels.
