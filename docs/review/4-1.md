## Assumptions & Adjustments
- **No-load prerequisite threshold (`torque <= T_rated * 0.15`)**: Prompt listed *"one row below 15 percent of rated current, one row near rated current"* alongside *"down to no load (belt released)"*. Because the 1.5 kW, 230 V induction motor's magnetising current alone draws `~3.36 A` (`56.7%` of `5.93 A` rated) with the brake belt completely released (`F = 0`), a line current below `0.15 × 5.93 = 0.89 A` is physically unachievable at rated voltage. The prerequisite therefore checks a near-no-load mechanical torque row (`torque <= 0.15 * T_rated`, satisfied cleanly at `F = 0 kgf`).
- **Signed torque in `solveSlipByBisection`**: Bisecting unclamped shaft torque `(Pag*(1-s) - Pfw)/omega_m` prevents clamping to zero below no-load slip, resolving exact no-load equilibrium at `s = 0.0016`, `IL = 3.36 A`, `Pin = 204 W`.
